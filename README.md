# Awesome Agentic & Vertical AI ERP

## The Ecosystem of Autonomous & Industry-Specific ERP Systems

**Curated List of SaaS Platforms & Open-Source Agentic ERP Projects**  
*Focused on Agentic Workflows, Multi-Agent Orchestration, and Vertical AI-Native Platforms*  
**Last updated: May 2026**

This repository tracks the evolution of Enterprise Resource Planning from "Systems of Record" to **"Systems of Action."** It features **Agentic ERP** platforms and **Vertical AI** solutions — industry-specific systems rebuilt around autonomous agents and intelligent workflows. These platforms go beyond traditional ERP by embedding **Multi-Agent Orchestration (MAO)** for:
- **Autonomous Reconciliation** (Finance/Accounting)
- **Predictive Supply Chain Intelligence** (Logistics/Distribution)
- **Intelligent Shop Floor Scheduling** (Manufacturing)
- **Automated Compliance & Risk Management** (Healthcare/Legal)
- **Zero-Touch Procurement** (Global Sourcing)

**Examples** include the category leaders: Epicor Kinetic, Infor CloudSuite, Plex, Dexciss, Rillet, Campfire, DevRev, and the emerging **ERPClaw**. These tools emphasize **agentic capabilities** (autonomous reasoning, self-reflection, and tool-use) and **vertical-specific AI modules**.

**Open-source emphasis**: This list includes every major active project for self-hosting, **Sovereign AI** deployment, and integration with modern agent frameworks (**LangGraph**, **CrewAI**, **Ollama**, etc.).

## 📚 Glossary of Agentic ERP Terms

- **Agentic ERP**: ERP systems that don't just record data but autonomously execute actions using AI agents.
- **Vertical AI**: AI models and systems trained and optimized for specific industry semantics (e.g., Manufacturing, Healthcare).
- **Multi-Agent Orchestration (MAO)**: The coordination of multiple specialized AI agents to solve cross-functional business problems.
- **Sovereign AI**: AI infrastructure and models that are hosted locally or within specific boundaries to ensure data privacy and compliance.
- **Headless ERP**: Decoupling the ERP backend from the UI to allow AI agents to interact directly via APIs.
