# Contributing to Awesome Agentic & Vertical AI ERP

We welcome contributions to this curated list of **Agentic ERP** and **Vertical AI** solutions. By contributing, you help the community discover industry-specific autonomous systems.

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork the Repository**: Create your own copy of the project.
2. **Add Your Entry**: Modify the `README.md` to include your suggested SaaS platform or open-source project.
   - Use the existing format: `[Name](Link) - Short description.`
   - Ensure the entry fits into one of the existing categories (Manufacturing, Finance, Healthcare, etc.).
   - If it's a new micro-vertical, feel free to suggest a new sub-category.
3. **Focus on Agentic Capabilities**: We prioritize tools that feature **Multi-Agent Orchestration**, **Autonomous Reasoning**, or **Industry-Specific AI Modules**.
4. **Submit a Pull Request**: Provide a brief explanation of why the tool should be included.

## Guidelines

- **Factual Descriptions**: Keep descriptions objective and focused on features.
- **Link Integrity**: Ensure all links are active and point to the official product or repository page.
- **Agentic Value**: Emphasize how the tool uses AI to move from "Systems of Record" to "Systems of Action."

## Search & LLM Optimization

When adding descriptions, try to use relevant keywords such as:
- Agentic Workflow
- Autonomous ERP Agent
- Vertical AI Module
- Multi-Agent Orchestration (MAO)
- Sovereign AI Deployment

Thank you for helping us build the most comprehensive list of **Vertical AI ERP** systems!
